Onboarding Risk Model

Scores each new account at its day-30 checkpoint from early usage signals, ranks the queue by risk, and tells the CSM which play to run first. All accounts and outcomes are synthetic.

How it works. Every signal is compared with a segment target (Enterprise and SMB differ) and turned into a shortfall from 0 to 1. The risk score is the weighted average shortfall, scaled to 100. Change the weights above and the queue and backtest both recalculate. Replace the synthetic data layer with a CSP or product-usage export to run it on real accounts.
